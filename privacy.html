Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Gratitude Key is a mindfulness app designed to help you build a daily gratitude habit. We take your privacy seriously — this policy explains exactly what we collect and what we don't.

Data We Collect

Your journal entries, mood selections, streak data, app preferences, and checkpoint settings are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal content.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any personally identifiable information. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or any third-party tracking. We do not sell your data — ever.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent to any server. You can disable them at any time in your device settings or inside the app.

Third-Party Services

Gratitude Key is built with Expo, a React Native framework. The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Data Storage & Security

All your data lives on your device. If you delete the app, all data is permanently deleted. We recommend using Settings → Export your journal if you want a backup before uninstalling.

Children's Privacy

Gratitude Key is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally, you have full control at all times. You can delete everything by uninstalling the app or using the reset option in Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the date above. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at gratitudekeyapp@gmail.com — we read every message.
